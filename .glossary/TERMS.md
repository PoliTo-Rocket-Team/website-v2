# PoliTo Rocket Team website domain vocabulary (TERMS)

The repo-owned vocabulary spine. One row per term: the canonical definition, and where a name has
drifted, what the term is **not**. When the code and this file disagree, the code is authoritative
and this file is the doc to fix.

## Team structure

| Term | Definition | Not |
|---|---|---|
| active member | A member who has not left: no leave date. "The Team" page lists active members ([design/project-notes.md](../design/project-notes.md), [design/HANDOFF.md](../design/HANDOFF.md)). The docs put joined/left dates on the member; in the schema the dates live on each role (`roles.started_at`, `roles.leaved_at`), not on `members`. | |
| alias address | A member's team email alias. It is their PRT email, `members.prt_email` ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts), epic #538, issue #563): Mark created on an alias request saves the address there, and a member may be asked for one when it is empty and no alias request is open. | A second address beside the PRT email. The old `members.email_alias` column was dropped by `drizzle/0075_drop_members_email_alias.sql` (issue #569). |
| alias request | A request for a team email alias for one member, open until it is marked created, declined or cancelled: `alias_requests` ([lib/dashboard/alias-request.ts](../lib/dashboard/alias-request.ts), epic #538). A lead, a head or the team leader sends it, or the site does by itself; the team leader, the operations lead or the IT lead answers it. One sent by hand carries the address the requester confirmed (`alias_requests.address`, issue #798); the dashboard calls it a personal PRT email request. | A hand-over request. |
| alumni | Former members. A member becomes alumni by Move to alumni (their roles end and their access goes; their account stays) or by leaving the team ([lib/dashboard/team.ts](../lib/dashboard/team.ts), [lib/dashboard/data.ts](../lib/dashboard/data.ts)). The dashboard lists them on its Alumni page and the public site on `/about/alumni`, by year ([app/about/alumni/page.tsx](../app/about/alumni/page.tsx)). | An active member. A deleted account. |
| board role | One of the two team-level seats flagged as full-access seats by `roles.full_access`: Project Manager and Chief Engineer (`BOARD_FLAGS` in [lib/dashboard/team.ts](../lib/dashboard/team.ts), migration 0047, issue #625). Read from the flag, never from the title, so a renamed one keeps its seat and its access. | A role type. Neither seat is a value of the role type enum. |
| stored title | A role's title as `roles.title` holds it, shown wherever a page names a head or a team-level seat by its role: Members, the Team tree, the Team page, Access and Close department (issue #625). A head row is written with the default "Head of <Department>" and can be renamed; a department rename rewrites only head titles still at the old default. The default is shown only for a blank title (`headTitleOf`, `boardTitleOf` in [lib/dashboard/team.ts](../lib/dashboard/team.ts)). | A label derived from the department or the seat. A title never decides a seat or a headship. |
| team-level seat | An active role in no department and no division that is not the team leader's, a head's or an advisor's (`isTeamSeat` in [lib/dashboard/team.ts](../lib/dashboard/team.ts), issue #625). The Team tree draws these seats in a row under the team leader: the flagged Project Manager, the flagged Chief Engineer, then any other by start date (`bySeatOrder`). Edit team tree changes who holds one and adds one ([lib/dashboard/team-seats.ts](../lib/dashboard/team-seats.ts), issue #627). | A full-access seat. Only the two board roles' flags give access; a further team-level seat gives none of its own. |
| department | The top unit of the team: `departments` table, with `name`, `code`, `started_at` and `closed_at` ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). A department with `closed_at` set is closed, and dashboard queries skip it. Code abbreviates it `dept` (`dept_id`, `dept_name`). | |
| division | A unit inside one department: `divisions` table, linked by `dept_id`, with its own `code` and `closed_at` ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). Apply positions, roles and scopes can point at a division. Code abbreviates it `div` (`div_id`, `div_name`). | |
| member | A person on the team: `members` table, keyed by `member_id`, holding team contact data (PRT email, mobile, Discord), NDA fields and a picture ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). A user becomes a member when `users.member` points at a member row. | A user. Every member is reached through a user, but most users (applicants) are not members. |
| member directory | The dashboard's list of members with their user data, roles, departments and divisions. About 150 rows; [0003](../.decisions/0003-neon-direct-pooled.md) requires it be read from a cache. | |
| NDA | The confidentiality agreement a member signs: `members.nda_signed_at`, `nda_name` (the name signed) and `nda_confirmed_by` (the member id of who confirmed it) ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). | |
| org structure | The tree of departments and their divisions, read once and cached under the `org-structure` cache tag ([app/actions/get-member-scopes.ts](../app/actions/get-member-scopes.ts)). | |
| PoliTo | Politecnico di Torino, the team's university. `users.polito_id` is the student's university id. | PRT. PoliTo is the university, PRT is the team. |
| PRT | PoliTo Rocket Team, the team itself. `members.prt_email` is the member's team address, separate from the personal `users.email`. | PoliTo. |
| role | One member's post in a department or division over a date range: `roles` table, with `title`, `type`, `started_at` and `leaved_at` ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). A member can hold many roles. | A scope (what a member may see or edit in the dashboard). An apply position (an opening to recruit for). |
| team address | A shared address on the team domain, such as a recruitment or logistics address, held by one member: `team_addresses` ([lib/dashboard/team-address.ts](../lib/dashboard/team-address.ts), epic #578). It stores what the address is for and, optionally, its moves-with role. One holder per address in any letter case. The member side view lists a member's team addresses in the PRT emails row, labelled "special" (board 102, issue #798); the dashboard calls one a special PRT email. | The PRT email (the alias address), which is one member's own address. |
| moves-with role | The role a team address follows when it changes hands: the team leader, a full-access seat (by its `roles.full_access` flag), a department's head, or a division's lead (`MovesWith` in [lib/dashboard/team-address.ts](../lib/dashboard/team-address.ts), grill #785). It reads as the org chart names the role, for example "Logistics Lead". | A free-text title. A role without a stable org reference, such as a titled seat with no full-access flag. |
| role type | The rank of a role: `president`, `head`, `lead` or `core`, on `roles.type`. The Postgres enum is named `position_type` (`positionTypeEnum`), though it types a role, not a position ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). | An apply position. The enum name says "position"; the thing it ranks is a role. |

## Accounts and access

| Term | Definition | Not |
|---|---|---|
| access level | How far a scope reaches on its target: `view` or `edit`, default `view` (`access_level_type` enum, [db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). | |
| Better Auth user | The sign-in identity in the `better_auth` Postgres schema: table `better_auth.user` (`betterAuthUsers`), with its sessions, accounts and verifications ([db/schema/better-auth.ts](../db/schema/better-auth.ts), [lib/auth.ts](../lib/auth.ts)). Sign-in is Google only; there is no email and password ([0008](../.decisions/0008-google-only-sign-in-no-email.md)). | A user. The Better Auth user is the login; the user is the profile row a trigger copies it into. |
| scope | One access grant to a member: `scopes` table, with a scope type (`admin`, `org`, `department`, `division`, `website`), a target, an access level, an optional department or division, and `given_by` (the member who granted it) ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). The column holding the scope type is also named `scope`. | A role. Also not the OAuth `scope` column on `better_auth.account`. |
| scope info | One member's scopes folded for a single target into flags (`hasAdminAccess`, `hasOrgAccess`, and their edit twins) and sets of department and division ids ([app/actions/get-member-scopes.ts](../app/actions/get-member-scopes.ts)). Dashboard reads filter by it. The `website` scope type is ignored when folding. | |
| scope rank | A ranking of scope types with a minimum per target, in `SCOPE_RANK` and `TARGET_MIN_REQUIREMENTS` ([lib/permission.config.ts](../lib/permission.config.ts)). Nothing imports it, and it lists `core_member`, which the `scope_type` enum does not have, while it lacks `website`. | Scope info, which is what the dashboard actually checks. |
| target | The dashboard area a scope covers: `all`, `positions`, `applications`, `members`, `orders`, `faq`, `blog` or `logs` (`target_type` enum, [db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). `all` matches every target. | |
| test developer | Retired (issue #612). It was a cookie sign-in on previews and under `next dev`, with no Google, no account and no database session, and a viewer switch on the user card ([0009](../.decisions/0009-previews-and-dev-use-dummy-data.md)). Checks now sign in as a tester and use View as, both real sessions. | A tester: a seeded seat or admin tester of `lib/seeded-accounts.ts` signed in through the tester sign-in (`lib/dev-tester.ts`, under `next dev` and on previews of the `preview` branch), which holds a real database session. |
| user | A person's profile row: `public.users`, with name, study data, PoliTo id, program, and an optional link to a member ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). A trigger, `sync_public_users_from_better_auth_user`, creates or updates it from the Better Auth user by id or email ([drizzle/0001_sync_better_auth_user_ids.sql](../drizzle/0001_sync_better_auth_user_ids.sql)). Applicants are users. | A Better Auth user. A member. |
| viewer | Who looks at the dashboard. Six kinds: non-member, alumni (off the team and on the Alumni record, #317), member, division lead, department head and operations lead, the full-access kind (`VIEWER_KINDS` in [lib/dashboard/viewer.ts](../lib/dashboard/viewer.ts)). The kind, with site-content access on top (`ViewerStanding`: a Content grant, which opens every content editor, the Alumni page included; there is no separate Alumni grant, #589), decides the sidebar and every page's content; `DASHBOARD_PAGES` in [lib/dashboard/access.ts](../lib/dashboard/access.ts) says which standing reaches which page. | A role or a scope: a real account's kind and site-content access are derived from its scopes and role (`viewerStandingOf`). |

## Recruiting

| Term | Definition | Not |
|---|---|---|
| added status | A status approvers add to the status list. It sits under one built-in status, its parent, chosen when it is created (for example "Second interview" under Interview), and behaves like it: the parent's public step and the parent's action buttons. It is set only by hand pick, and can be deleted only while no application uses it ([0011](../.decisions/0011-applicant-journey-received-to-joining.md), status-list amendment). | A built-in status. An action button never sets an added status. |
| application | One user's request to join through one apply position: `applications` table, with a CV, a motivation letter, custom answers and an application status ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). It is sent through the apply form on a public position's page `/apply/[slug]` ([components/apply/application-form.tsx](../components/apply/application-form.tsx)), with PDFs of at most 2 MB (`MAX_PDF_BYTES` in [lib/apply/application-form.ts](../lib/apply/application-form.ts), [0010](../.decisions/0010-uploads-in-two-blob-stores.md)). Its journey from received to joining is [0011](../.decisions/0011-applicant-journey-received-to-joining.md). | |
| application file | An uploaded CV or motivation letter: `application_files` table, with original filename, type, size and hash ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). The bytes live in the private Vercel Blob store under `applications/`; the row keeps their pathname (column `r2_key`, an old name) and the file is served only through an access-checked route ([lib/storage/private-store.ts](../lib/storage/private-store.ts)). An application points at up to two of them. | |
| application status | Where an application stands, from one status list: one of the ten built-in statuses or an added status (Owner decisions on epic #588; [0011](../.decisions/0011-applicant-journey-received-to-joining.md), status-list amendment). Anyone who can move the application sets it, by an action button or a hand pick in the Status picker, and both run one server move; only the team leader sets "Accepted · email sent". The applicant never sees it, only its public step. It is stored as `applications.status_key`, a reference into the `application_statuses` list; the old `application_status` enum column stays in place until a later cleanup drops it ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). Until then every move except a hand pick of an added status still writes it beside the reference, and a few places still read it: the move's check that the application has not changed since it was read, the Members page's joining list, and Delete account's check for open applications. | A stage: there are no separate stages and statuses, and "stage" is retired as an approver-facing word for applications. "In review", which is no status. Order status, an unrelated enum whose Postgres name is just `status`. |
| apply position | A recruiting opening in one division: `apply_positions` table (`ApplyPosition` in code), with title, description, required and desirable skills, custom questions and whether a motivation letter is required ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). `status` is a boolean (true means active) and `is_deleted` hides it for good. The dashboard route, the scope target and the UI all say "position". | A role or role type. |
| built-in status | One of the ten statuses every application can have, in order: New; Interview; Accepted · email to be sent; Accepted · email sent; NDA received; Joined; Rejected · email to be sent; Rejected · email sent; Accepted for another position; Withdrawn. They can be renamed and recoloured but never deleted, and the action buttons only ever set them ([0011](../.decisions/0011-applicant-journey-received-to-joining.md), status-list amendment). | An added status. "In review", which is not one of them. |
| confirm join | The step that adds an accepted applicant to the position's division: the `confirm-join` move in [lib/dashboard/application-flow.ts](../lib/dashboard/application-flow.ts) and `confirmJoin` in [lib/dashboard/data.ts](../lib/dashboard/data.ts). It runs from "NDA received", the status that records the day the signed NDA arrived, and makes someone new or coming back a member ([0011](../.decisions/0011-applicant-journey-received-to-joining.md)). Someone already on the team follows the same statuses as any applicant, "NDA received" included, then joins the new division and keeps every other one ([0014](../.decisions/0014-several-divisions-one-nda.md), its latest amendment). Under the status list, Confirm join sets "Joined" and the person's other open applications to "Accepted for another position" ([0011](../.decisions/0011-applicant-journey-received-to-joining.md), status-list amendment). The ruling gives it to the lead or the recruitment manager, who is the Logistics lead and may confirm join team-wide, in every division, by the same Applications rule (`managesRecruitment`, #194). The Applications area decides who may, on the Applications page and on the Members page's joining banner alike: the rule every move on an application asks (#584). | Accept, which only marks the application accepted. |
| custom question | A free-text question an apply position asks (`apply_positions.custom_questions`); the applicant's replies are stored on the application as `custom_answers` ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). | |
| joining | An accepted applicant not yet on the team, waiting for the signed NDA. Someone already on the team is never joining: they are a member already, even while their new application goes through "NDA received" like any other ([0014](../.decisions/0014-several-divisions-one-nda.md), its latest amendment). The statuses "Accepted · email to be sent", "Accepted · email sent" and "NDA received" (`AcceptedStatus` in [lib/dashboard/application-flow.ts](../lib/dashboard/application-flow.ts)), listed to the division lead as `Joining` ([lib/dashboard/team.ts](../lib/dashboard/team.ts)). The applicant sees only "Check your email." ([0011](../.decisions/0011-applicant-journey-received-to-joining.md), its 2026-10-27 amendment). | A member. Accept does not make anyone a member; only confirm join does. |
| motivation letter | The letter file an application may carry, required when the apply position sets `requires_motivation_letter`. Code also calls it `ml` (`ml_name`) and, in the foreign key, cover letter (`cover_letter_file_id`); all three are one file. | A separate cover letter. There is only one letter. |
| open position | An apply position with `status` true and not deleted: its lead opened it ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)). Open is not enough to show it: the public `/apply` page reads public positions, which also need the recruitment switch on (`isPublic` in [lib/apply/positions.ts](../lib/apply/positions.ts), [app/actions/get-apply-positions.ts](../app/actions/get-apply-positions.ts)). | Any apply position. Inactive and deleted ones are not open. |
| placeholder role | A role the team usually recruits for, shown on `/apply` marked closed when no position is public: all of them with none public, none with one or more public. Written in code, never read from the database (`placeholderRoles` in [lib/apply/placeholder-roles.ts](../lib/apply/placeholder-roles.ts), `applyListing` in [lib/apply/positions.ts](../lib/apply/positions.ts), [0013](../.decisions/0013-apply-placeholders-and-position-visibility.md)). | An apply position. A placeholder role has no row and takes no applications. |
| public position | An apply position shown on the public site: its lead opened it, it is not deleted, and the recruitment switch is on (`isPublic` in [lib/apply/positions.ts](../lib/apply/positions.ts), [0013](../.decisions/0013-apply-placeholders-and-position-visibility.md)). | An open position, which its lead opened but which is hidden while the recruitment switch is off. |
| public step | What the applicant sees of an application on My applications, never its status: Received, In review (every status in between), Interview, the outcome (Accepted only from "Accepted · email sent"; Not selected only from "Rejected · email sent" and for "Accepted for another position") and Withdrawn ([0011](../.decisions/0011-applicant-journey-received-to-joining.md), status-list amendment). An added status shows its parent's public step. | An application status. "In review" is a public step only; approvers never see it as a status. |
| recruitment switch | The site-wide on/off for recruitment, flipped on the dashboard by anyone with full access: every full-access seat, or an org or admin scope row, with no positions grant needed (issues #121, #621). Off, no position is public. Stored as the one row of `recruitment_setting` (`is_open`, default on) ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts)); who may flip it is `canSwitchRecruitment` in [lib/apply/recruitment-switch.ts](../lib/apply/recruitment-switch.ts). | A position's own open/closed status, which its lead sets. |

## Public site

| Term | Definition | Not |
|---|---|---|
| dashboard | The members' area behind sign-in, under `/dashboard`: applications, positions and members ([design/project-notes.md](../design/project-notes.md)). The shell and Overview are `app/dashboard/` (issue #141); its other pages still sit in the legacy route group. | |
| department group | A heading on the Team page that holds department blocks, such as "Technical Departments" or "Operations". The Team page editor creates, renames, orders and deletes groups, and refuses to delete one that still holds departments; each department block sits under exactly one group (epic [#835](https://github.com/PoliTo-Rocket-Team/website-v2/issues/835)). | A department, or the `departments.kind` column. The Team tree no longer sets a department's group. |
| edit shell | The dashboard frame a content editor opens an item in: the item's real page under an edit bar ("<Section> / Editing <item>", its Draft, Draft changes or Published state, Desktop \| Phone, Save draft, Publish, and a page switch when the item shows on more than one page), with each named block of the page always outlined, with its name and an Edit chip that opens that block's section of the edit panel ([components/dashboard/edit-shell.tsx](../components/dashboard/edit-shell.tsx), [lib/content/edit-shell.ts](../lib/content/edit-shell.ts)). It knows no content type: Outreach, Projects and the Team page each hand it their own page, blocks and sections. | The edit panel. The shell is the page and bar around it; the panel is the side form it opens. |
| page seat | A place the Team page's stored layout shows: the team leader, a place on the arc, a department block's head or one of its lead rows. Each page seat holds one seat binding and shows whoever holds that seat of the team record now. The first fill writes a layer version's first layout; after that the Team page editor adds, removes or moves page seats, with two Team tree exceptions: a hand-over moves the page seats bound to the old `roles` row onto the new row, and deleting a team seat removes the page seats bound to it. Adding, moving or reordering a seat in the Team tree adds or moves no page seat. A page seat whose bound seat has no holder is not shown, on the page or in the editor, and keeps its place (epic [#835](https://github.com/PoliTo-Rocket-Team/website-v2/issues/835)). | A seat of the team record, or a person. Not a spot either: a spot is built from the roles, a page seat is stored in the layout. There is no vacant or unbound page seat. |
| partner | A company whose logo runs in the Partners section and on `/partners` ([components/landing/partners.tsx](../components/landing/partners.tsx)). The logo files sit in folders named `sponsors/`, and [0005](../.decisions/0005-vercel-hobby-accept-non-commercial-clause.md) says "sponsor logos": both mean partner. Static list for now. | A separate sponsor concept. Sponsor is the old name for the same thing. |
| post | An Outreach post: one event the team took part in, with a title, date, optional place, tags, a lead and card summary, text blocks, photos (the first is the cover and card photo) and links to the original posts. Site-content editors write it in the dashboard, and it lives in the content store with a Draft or Published state ([db/schema/public.ts](../db/schema/public.ts) `outreach_posts`, [lib/dashboard/database-outreach.ts](../lib/dashboard/database-outreach.ts)). Only Published posts show, on `/outreach`, on the post's own page, and as the newest cards in the home Track record section ([lib/outreach-public.ts](../lib/outreach-public.ts), [components/landing/latest.tsx](../components/landing/latest.tsx)). Tags are data (`outreach_tags`), so editors can add one; the tag marked Award is the one whose posts the `/outreach` header counts as awards. | A draft. Only the Published version of a post shows on the public site. |
| project | One of the team's engineering programmes: Cavour, VES and Efesto. There are three: VES Mark I and Mark II are versions of one project, and Efesto is an engine, not a vehicle ([design/specs-from-old-site.md](../design/specs-from-old-site.md), [design/HANDOFF.md](../design/HANDOFF.md)). The landing cards and footer still show four entries. | A vehicle. A version. |
| seat binding | The reference to a seat of the team record that a page seat holds: the team leader; a full-access seat, by its flag; any other team-level seat, by its `roles` row; a department head, by department; or a division lead, by division and place among its co-leads. A hand-over of a team-level seat with no flag starts a new `roles` row, and the same write moves the bindings onto it (epic [#835](https://github.com/PoliTo-Rocket-Team/website-v2/issues/835)). | A binding to a person. The page seat follows whoever holds the seat. |
| visual layer | The stored Team page edits: what `/about/the-team` shows on top of the page the team's roles build, with a published version and a draft ([lib/about/team-layer.ts](../lib/about/team-layer.ts), [0015](../.decisions/0015-team-page-reads-roles.md)). It never changes a role, division, department or access. Reset to team clears it except the intro text and the group photo. | A role change. Editing the layer does not change who leads in the dashboard. |
| spot | A place on the Team page a person fills: the team leader, a place on the arc, a head block or a division lead row. Visual layer edits are keyed to spots, so they outlive changes to the real team ([lib/about/team-layer.ts](../lib/about/team-layer.ts)). | A role or a person. |
| Team page advisor | A faculty member or specialist outside the student team, listed in the Team page's Advisors section as a principal advisor or a specialist. Added by hand in the Team page editor and kept as content items with drafts, never built from roles ([lib/about/advisors.ts](../lib/about/advisors.ts)). End advising moves them to the Alumni page's Advisors group with their role and years. Reset to team never changes them. | The Advisor role a member holds after a hand-over (#253), which is a role in a division. |
| vehicle | A rocket that flies within a project: Cavour and VES. Efesto has no vehicle ([design/specs-from-old-site.md](../design/specs-from-old-site.md)). | A project. |
| version | One build of a vehicle: VES Test Version, Mark I and Mark II. Cavour's builds are called configs instead (CVR 100-75-3, -75-4, -54-6) ([design/specs-from-old-site.md](../design/specs-from-old-site.md)). | A project. |
